gsheet privacy policy

gsheet runs only on the owner's own computer. It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, which gives it access only to files that gsheet itself created in the owner's Drive.

Spreadsheet contents and the access token travel only between the owner's computer and Google's servers. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any data, and no data is sent to any third-party server.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
